🌐 Web Tool ● Live

SustainMetrics — GHG Calculator & Emission Factor API

Accurate data. Instant calculations. Professional reports.

A carbon accounting platform that simplifies Scope 1, 2, and 3 emission calculations using official government datasets. Free tier available; Pro and Business unlock cloud projects, API access, and advanced reporting.

What's inside

  • 4 official data sources. UK DEFRA 2025, US EPA 2025, ADEME Base Carbone, Ember global electricity (193 countries).
  • Cloud projects & dashboard. Save inventories, track trends over time, manage multiple projects from one place.
  • PDF & Excel export. Generate professional carbon reports. Compare emission factors across sources and years.

Who it's for

Sustainability professionals, ESG consultants, environmental engineers, and teams preparing carbon footprint reports.
